# How This Course Is Made

Authors map sections to the official Certified Kubernetes Security Specialist (CKS) objectives and check mechanisms against primary documentation. They review examples for responsible components, prerequisites and meaningful failure cases.

Structural checks verify reading order, section introductions, closing summaries and YAML question contracts. On every change, continuous integration starts each changed lab and playground. It solves each lab with its reference solution and checks that every graded outcome passes. The output that the solutions quote comes from these live runs.

The [contributors](https://github.com/astrona-io/ATS033/graphs/contributors) maintain the material. To [report a mistake](https://github.com/astrona-io/ATS033/issues), identify the page and disputed statement. The [license](https://github.com/astrona-io/ATS033/blob/main/LICENSE) governs reuse.

Objectives belong to their respective owners. Teaching and knowledge checks are original practice material. Application names are illustrative and have no special technical meaning.
